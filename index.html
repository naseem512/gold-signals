      Signal ke rules
      • BUY: EMA50 > EMA200 aur price EMA200 se upar, EMA9 EMA21 ko neeche se cross kare, RSI 50–70.
      • SELL: EMA50 < EMA200 aur price EMA200 se neeche, EMA9 EMA21 ko upar se cross kare, RSI 30–50.
      • Active mode mein ye bhi: trend chal raha ho, price wapas EMA21 tak aaye aur candle trend ki taraf band ho (pullback entry).
      • Fake-signal filter: 4H trend same taraf, signal candle mazboot (body > 50%), price EMA21 se 1 ATR ke andar.
      • Signal sirf band ho chuki 1H candle par banta hai, baad mein badalta nahi.
      • SL = 2.5 × ATR. TP1 = 1R, TP2 = 3R. Trade 72 ghante baad khud band.
      • Weekend (market band) ka data hata diya jata hai.
